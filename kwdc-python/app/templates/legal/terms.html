KTM-WDC Commercial Terms of Service

Operated under Nepal Commercial Code & Transport Regulations

1. Warehousing Storage Agreements

All warehouse space reservations are subject to physical verification and mutual contract signoff. Tenants storing goods in cold storage or ambient godowns must declare hazardous or perishable contents prior to batch ingestion.

2. Freight Transportation & Transit Liability

Drivers operate under carrier agreements with verified vehicle documentation (blue book, pollution certificates, insurance). Damage claims must be reported within 24 hours of consignee handover with accompanying Digital Proof of Delivery (POD) photos.

3. Heavy Equipment & Operator Protocols

Machinery leased through the portal (cranes, forklifts) is operated by certified technicians. Daily hire rates, operator allowances, and fuel provisions adhere to agreed platform quotes.
