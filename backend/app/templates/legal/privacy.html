Nepal Logistics Statutory Compliance & Data Privacy

Last Updated: November 2026 | Regulated by the Laws of Nepal

1. Regulatory Jurisdiction

KTM-WDC operates in strict conformity with the Electronic Transactions Act (ETA) 2063, Individual Privacy Act 2075, and Nepal Value Added Tax Act 2052. All freight waybills, digital proofs of delivery, and electronic tax invoices conform with standards prescribed by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD).

2. Realtime Telemetry & Location Data

GPS telemetry streamed by commercial drivers is processed solely during active dispatch routes to facilitate cargo tracking, consignee delivery time estimates, and administrative route audits. Coordinate histories are cryptographically secured and purged in accordance with data retention mandates.

3. IRD Tax Reporting & VAT Integrity

All commercial warehousing leases, freight transport, and heavy machinery services attract statutory 13% Value Added Tax (VAT). Tax invoices are generated sequentially with tamper-evident QR verification tokens.
