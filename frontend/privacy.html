Nepal Logistics Statutory Compliance & Data Privacy

Regulated by the Laws of Nepal | Electronic Transactions Act 2063

1. Regulatory Jurisdiction

KTM-WDC operates in strict conformity with the Electronic Transactions Act (ETA) 2063, Individual Privacy Act 2075, and Nepal Value Added Tax Act 2052. All freight waybills, digital proofs of delivery, and electronic tax invoices conform with standards prescribed by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD).

2. Realtime Telemetry & Location Data

Driver GPS telemetry is transmitted via secure ASGI WebSockets and utilized solely for active freight tracking, delivery ETA calculations, and dispute arbitration.
