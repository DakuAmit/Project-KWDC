Master Commercial Lease Agreement Terms

Standard legal covenants executed with KTM-WDC Pvt. Ltd. upon physical facility verification.

1. Exclusivity & Lockout: Once a property is audited and approved, feature modifications, capacity alterations, and tenant assignments are governed by KTM-WDC to prevent arbitrary evictions or unexpected tariff hikes.

2. Maintenance Hold Protocol: If urgent structural repairs, electrical rewiring, or shutter maintenance are needed, the owner can trigger a Maintenance Hold with a mandatory 10+ character explanation note.

3. Holding Covenant: Prior to lease assignment, KTM-WDC bears Rs. 0 holding liability. The owner is free to request delisting with 15 days written notice if unleased.

4. Nepal IRD Tax & VAT: Commercial rentals comply with statutory Inland Revenue Department Schedule 13 electronic invoicing. 10% platform fee is levied on gross monthly contract turnover.
